Slate & Ice Design System

A calm, professional UI language for data-heavy dashboards. Deep navy structure, ice-blue surfaces and a single warm coral accent — on soft, rounded cards.

layers

Navy builds structure

Navy is the one structural colour: sidebar, hero, panel headers, primary action, selected state.

ac_unit

Ice keeps it light

Ice-blue tints carry selection, soft buttons and inset blocks. Content lives on white cards.

bolt

Coral is rare

One warm accent for the active tab, a key metric, a CTA or a chart series — never decoration.

crop_square

Soft, round, airy

Radii grow with size, shadows barely show, and 24px of air separates every card.

04 //

Radius & elevation

Radii step up with the size of the element. Shadows are faint and navy-tinted; depth comes mostly from white cards on an ice surface.

05 //

Iconography

Material Symbols Rounded, filled, weight 500. 20px default · 18px in buttons and chips · 16px inline. Navy for actions on white, ice-on-navy on dark surfaces.

06 //

Motion

Quick and quiet. One easing curve, three durations. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Fast120ms

Press states, checkmarks, icon swaps.

Base200ms

Hovers, toggles, colour changes, card lift.

Slow320ms

Modals, toasts, progress fills. Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1).
